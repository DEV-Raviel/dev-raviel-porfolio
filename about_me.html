Curiosity & The Hacker Mindset

My fascination with cybersecurity started young with a simple, ambitious goal: trying to crack a neighbor's Wi-Fi network. Armed with nothing but an Android phone and endless internet searches, that early curiosity transformed me into an obsessive tech tinkerer.

In high school, structured security resources were scarce, making ethical hacking feel like an impossible discipline. But in college, studying networking protocols and communications laid down the exact mathematical and architectural fundamentals I needed—reigniting my passion into penetration testing and offensive security.

This is my story — from Android tinkering and Photoshop experiments to C++ engineering and UI/UX design.

From Photoshop as a hobby to UI/UX

Parallel to my technical deep dives, high school was where my visual creative journey began. Driven by a desire to master Photoshop, I spent hours experimenting with graphic composition, lighting effects, and visual layouts.

Over time, that visual discipline expanded into CSS graphic design and interface design. Crafting this personal website became the turning point where my background in penetration testing, C++ performance, and UI/UX styling merged into one cohesive craft.
